The mark

The lockup is the written word and the caret: cue followed by the next line. The caret is part of the mark — never drop it, move it, or animate it in static use.

Single color only: #1C1C1E on light, #ECECEF on dark. Brand ink #2C2C31 / #E6E6EA is also allowed.

Clear space

x x x x

Keep clear space of x = the x-height of the wordmark on every side. For the symbol alone, ¼ of its size.

Minimum sizes

Lockup 18 px tall · 7 mm print
Symbol 16 px
32 px

Below 18 px tall, use the symbol. The symbol is drawn on a 1.5-unit grid so it lands on whole pixels at 16, 24, 32 and 48 px; scale it in those steps on screen.

App icon

  • Paper body #FAFAFB → #ECECEF; ink #2C2C31 (written line @ 28%, rules @ 10%).
  • Dark body #232327 → #141416; ink #E6E6EA (written @ 30%, rules @ 12%).
  • At 16 and 32 px use the simplified icon: current line + caret only.
  • Never place the mark or a hue on the icon; never redraw it with a gloss.
