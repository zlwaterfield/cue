Accounts and privacy

No account needed

Each app is complete on its own. Your notes live on your Mac or iPhone, and without an account nothing you write ever leaves it.

An account for sync

To have the same notes on your Mac and iPhone, turn on sync on both and sign in with Apple or your email. The notes already on each one come along.

No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no ads and nothing sold. Cue doesn't know how you use it, and your notes are only ever used to sync them back to you.

Free, and staying that way

No paid plan, no trial, no credit card, nothing to unlock. Sync is free too. Cue is a personal project rather than a business, and I don’t plan to charge for it.

Assistants, if you want one

With sync on, Claude or ChatGPT can read and edit your days over MCP. Nothing it does can delete a thing, and there’s no AI inside the app itself.

Yours to take, or delete

Export everything as Markdown any time. Turning sync off keeps every note on your Mac or iPhone, and deleting your account in Settings deletes it from the server.

Cue is open source, so you can read exactly what it does, build it yourself or run your own sync server. It's all on GitHub.
