Every day is a page.

Daily notes and to-dos for Mac and iOS.
Fast, quiet and keyboard-first.

Why I built it

I wanted one place to put the day. Not a project manager or a second brain, just a page for today that I could open and type into without deciding where anything goes first.

Every tool I tried wanted a project, a tag or a priority before it would take a note. So I built Cue, for myself: days are the only structure, the keyboard does everything, and it's ready before I've finished thinking of what to write.

What makes it different

The day is the only folder

Things belong to dates, not projects. No tags, priorities or dashboards. Whatever didn't get done moves to tomorrow with ⌘D.

Keyboard first

Tab nests, ⌘↩ completes, and ⌘K does everything else, including dates like “move to fri”. The mouse still works.

Fast, and measured

Ready to type 240 ms after launch, even with five years of notes. A keystroke renders in about 2 ms. Every number has a budget and a test.

On your Mac and your iPhone

Saved as you type, with no telemetry and Markdown export any time. The Mac needs no account; turn on sync and the same notes are on your iPhone.

Cue stays small on purpose: no reminders, recurring tasks, collaboration or AI features.

Where it's at

It's in beta. Cue is my daily driver on my Mac and my iPhone, and it runs without any issues. There are no downloads yet, so for now you build it yourself.

One day I'll launch it properly. For now it's a fun little thing I use every day. If you're curious, the code is open and you can build it yourself.

A hosted version?

Right now, using Cue means building it yourself, and syncing it to your iPhone means running your own Supabase server. I'm open to a hosted version if there's enough interest: sign in with your email, and your notes are on your Mac and your iPhone, with nothing to set up. It would be free, or cost very little.

If you'd use it, give the waitlist issue on GitHub a thumbs-up, and subscribe to it to hear if it happens. Nothing is promised to a date.
